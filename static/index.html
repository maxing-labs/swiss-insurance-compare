    About Priminfo Pro

    Data source

    All premiums come live and unaltered from priminfo.admin.ch, the official premium calculator of the Swiss Federal Office of Public Health (FOPH / BAG). They are refreshed once the FOPH publishes the next year's premiums at the end of September.

    Independent · No ads

    No advertising, no commissions from insurers, no tracking. Saved profiles stay in your own browser and are never uploaded.

    How it is calculated

    Total annual cost = 12 × net monthly premium + your medical bills up to the franchise + 10% of the bills above it (capped at CHF 700 per year for adults, CHF 350 for children). Net premiums already deduct the monthly environmental levy rebate.

    For guidance only

    Results are for guidance only and are neither an offer nor insurance advice. The insurer's quote and priminfo.admin.ch are authoritative: check there before switching. Basic insurance (KVG / LAMal) covers the same benefits with every insurer by law; the switching deadline is usually the last working day of November.
